Hide from Krampus Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Hide from Krampus ("the game") is a Christmas hide-and-seek game for iPhone made by Phong Bui ("I", "me"). The game is made for children and families. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. You don't need an account to play, and the game never asks for a name, email address, photo, location or any other personal details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: your coins, which maps you have unlocked and won, the clothes, costumes and pets you own and wear, whether you play as a boy or a girl, your sound and camera settings, and which purchases you own. This stays on your phone. I do not receive it. If you delete the game, it is removed.

The game has no chat, no friends list, no accounts and no way to share anything with other players. The other children in the game (Holly, Jasper and Noel) are computer-controlled characters.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads that a player chooses to watch (for example for extra coins or a second chance), and an occasional ad between rounds.

Because the game is made for children, every ad request is marked as directed at children (under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent (for the EU's GDPR). This means:

  • ads are not personalised and are not based on a player's interests or past activity;
  • only ads rated suitable for general audiences (G) are requested;
  • the game does not ask for permission to track you on iPhone and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

To deliver ads, limit how often they appear and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as your IP address and basic device and app information. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's guidance on child-directed apps.

Purchases

The game offers optional purchases: map unlocks and packs of in-game coins. Before any purchase, the game shows a grown-up check (a times-table question) so that children can't buy things by accident. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. I do not see or store payment details. Apple tells the game whether a purchase has been made, so it can be delivered, and so map unlocks can be restored on a new device. Everything else in the game (clothes, costumes and pets) is bought with coins earned by playing.

Children

The game is designed for children. I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The game does not contain analytics or tracking tools and does not link to outside websites except this policy. If you are a parent and have a question about your child's privacy, please contact me at the address below.

Sharing

I do not sell or share your information. The only parties that receive any data because of the game are Google (to show ads, as described above) and Apple (for purchases).

Your choices

  • Play without watching rewarded ads: they are always optional.
  • Turn on Screen Time or Ask to Buy on your child's iPhone to control purchases.
  • Delete the game to remove everything it stores on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date above.
