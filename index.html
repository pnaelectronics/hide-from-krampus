Hide from Krampus

It's Christmas Eve. The lights are out. Krampus is coming.

Every Christmas Eve, Santa visits the good children. But Santa has a grumpy, horned partner: Krampus, who comes for kids who are still awake after the lights go out. Only one thing scares him away: a fully lit Christmas tree.

How to play

  • Hide in wardrobes, under beds and behind curtains before Krampus comes in.
  • Finish the mission on each map to light up Christmas and send Krampus running.
  • Throw presents to trick him, sneak to stay quiet, and listen for his growl.
  • Rescue your friends: caught friends end up in Krampus's sack. Creep over and set them free!

Five spooky maps, five missions

  • Winterbourne Mansion, Ground Floor: fix the blown fuses
  • Upper Floor: find the lost Christmas baubles
  • The Whole House: light the candles on every floor, where Krampus is angrier and faster
  • Grandma Ginny's Gingerbread Cottage: find her cookies in the snowy woods
  • The Snowy Village: light the lanterns as you sneak between four houses

Dress up, with your pet by your side

Earn coins to buy hundreds of hats, tops, costumes and festive extras, and mix and match them however you like. Every outfit is yours to keep. Your pet follows you everywhere and even hides with you. Beat every map to earn the legendary Hero Puppy.

A new surprise every day

  • Advent calendar: open a door every day from 1 to 24 December for coins and special outfits
  • Daily tasks: three new challenges every day
  • Daily gift: keep your streak going all year round
