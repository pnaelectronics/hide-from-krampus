Hide from Krampus

It's Christmas Eve. The lights are out. Krampus is coming.

Every Christmas Eve, Santa visits the good children. But Santa has a grumpy, horned partner: Krampus, who comes for kids who are still awake after the lights go out. Only one thing scares him away: a fully lit Christmas tree.

How to play

  • Hide in cupboards, under beds and behind sofas before Krampus comes in.
  • Fix the 3 blown fuses to light up the Christmas tree and send Krampus running.
  • Throw presents to trick him, sneak to stay quiet, and listen for growls.

Five spooky maps

  • Ground Floor and Upper Floor of the old Winterbourne Mansion
  • The Whole House, where Krampus is angrier and faster
  • Grandma Ginny's Gingerbread Cottage in the snowy woods
  • The Snowy Village, with four houses to sneak between

Dress up and make friends

Choose a boy or a girl and earn coins to buy hundreds of outfits, hats, costumes and pets. Your pet follows you everywhere and hides with you. Beat every map to earn the legendary Hero Puppy.
